Burger

A mouthwatering burger made with a juicy, perfectly seasoned beef patty, layered with fresh lettuce, ripe tomatoes, onions, melted cheese, and delicious special sauce, all served in a soft, toasted bun.

Ingredients

  • Ground beef patty
  • Burger bun
  • Tomato
  • Cheese
  • Lettuce

Steps

  1. Prepare and season the ground beef patty.
  2. Cook the patty until fully cooked.
  3. Toast the burger buns lightly.
  4. Add lettuce, tomato, cheese, and the cooked patty.
  5. Add special sauce, close the bun, and serve.
