Chicken

A delicious and satisfying chicken meal featuring tender, juicy chicken seasoned with aromatic herbs and spices, cooked to perfection. Served with fluffy rice or crispy fries, fresh vegetables, and a flavorful sauce. Every bite offers a perfect balance of rich flavor, tenderness, and freshness.

Ingredients

  • Chicken
  • Garlic
  • Onion
  • Black pepper
  • Cooking oil

Steps

  1. Wash and prepare the chicken.
  2. Season with garlic, onion, black pepper, and salt.
  3. Heat oil in a pan.
  4. Cook the chicken until fully cooked and golden brown.
  5. Serve hot with rice, vegetables, or fries.
