Lasagna

A delicious Italian-inspired dish made with layers of tender pasta sheets, rich and savory meat sauce, creamy béchamel, and melted cheese. Baked to golden perfection, every slice is warm, cheesy, flavorful, and satisfying.

Ingredients

  • Lasagna pasta sheets
  • Ground beef
  • Tomato sauce
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Béchamel sauce

Steps

  1. Cook the lasagna pasta sheets according to the package instructions.
  2. Prepare the ground beef with tomato sauce and spices.
  3. Prepare the creamy béchamel sauce.
  4. Layer pasta, meat sauce, béchamel, and cheese in a baking dish.
  5. Bake until golden, bubbly, and fully cooked.
