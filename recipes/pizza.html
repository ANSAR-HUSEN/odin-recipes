Pizza

A delicious, freshly baked pizza topped with rich tomato sauce, melted mozzarella cheese, and a tasty combination of fresh vegetables, savory toppings, and aromatic herbs. Baked to perfection with a crispy crust and a soft, cheesy center.

Ingredients

  • Bell pepper
  • Pepperoni
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Pizza dough
  • Tomato sauce

Steps

  1. Prepare the pizza dough.
  2. Spread tomato sauce evenly over the dough.
  3. Add mozzarella cheese and your favorite toppings.
  4. Bake in a preheated oven until the crust is golden and the cheese melts.
  5. 1. Slice and serve hot.
